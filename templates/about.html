About the project

Predictive Healthcare & Disease Risk Prediction

B.Tech CSE (7th Semester) major project — Samrat Ashok Rajkiya Engineering College, Mirzapur.

The problem

Chronic diseases like diabetes, heart and kidney disease progress silently. Lab reports are full of numbers that are hard for a non-medical person to interpret, and access to specialists is limited in semi-urban and rural regions like Mirzapur.

Our solution

A unified web portal that scans a medical report (OCR), cleans the data, runs trained ML classifiers and returns an explainable risk estimate with recommendations — all stored per patient in a database.
